Privacy Policy

R.S. Global Academy App

Effective date: 20 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the R.S. Global Academy mobile application (“App”) may handle information when students, parents/guardians, teachers, staff, or other authorised users access it. The App is intended to support school-related communication and services.

Important: The school must ensure this policy matches the App’s actual features, database, permissions, and third-party services before publishing it. Remove any data category or feature below that the App does not use, and add any actual service provider that processes personal information.

1. Information That May Be Processed

Depending on the features enabled and the information entered by the school or user, the App may process:

  • Student details such as name, class, roll number, and school identifier.
  • Academic information such as attendance, homework, assessments, examination marks, and report cards.
  • Parent or guardian details, such as name, phone number, and communication preferences.
  • Teacher or staff details needed for authorised school functions.
  • Account information needed to sign in and control access.
  • Technical, diagnostic, or security information, if collected by the App or its hosting and service providers.

The school should collect and process only information reasonably needed for the App’s stated school-related purposes.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide school services, display academic and attendance records to authorised users, share notices and schedules, support school-parent communication, maintain account security, troubleshoot issues, and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Student and Child Privacy

The App may be used in connection with students under 18. Student information should be treated as confidential and access should be limited according to user roles and school permissions. Parents or legal guardians should contact the school for questions about a child’s information or to request a correction, subject to applicable law and school record-retention requirements.

4. Sharing of Information

The school does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be accessible to authorised school personnel and may be processed by service providers used to host, secure, maintain, or operate the App. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to protect users and the school. The school should list relevant providers here if they receive personal information.

5. Storage and Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as needed for school operations, legitimate educational purposes, and applicable legal or record-retention requirements. To request access, correction, or deletion where applicable, contact the school using the details below. The school may need to verify the requester’s identity and authority before acting.

7. Permissions and Notifications

If the App requests device permissions or sends notifications, those features should be used only for their stated purpose. Users can review available app-permission and notification controls in their device settings. This section should be updated if the App accesses location, camera, microphone, contacts, files, or other sensitive device features.

8. Third-Party Services and Links

The App may rely on third-party hosting, authentication, analytics, messaging, or notification services if configured by the school. Those providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Any third-party service actually used by the App should be identified here before publication.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App, its information practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version should be published at the same public URL. The effective date at the top will be updated when material changes are made.
